## 1. STRATEGY

**What we are launching:** IMAGINARIUM OS — a bundled offer: the **Accelerator 363** 90-day program (Skool community, weekly live labs, cohort projects, hackathon finale) **plus** the **OS Toolkit** delivered inside it (AI prompt libraries, automation templates, tool guides — the "Community Tool Shed" from the PLANS doc). One price, one door in.

**Pricing (from the PLANS doc, confirmed):** $121/month, or $444 one-time for the full 90 days (positioned as "save $50 + founding status").

**The single most important outcome:** **75 paying members enrolled by Day 90** (target range 50–100). At an average of ~$400/member that is roughly $30K, and — more importantly — a full first cohort that produces testimonials, hackathon results, and re-enrollment proof for cohort 2.

**The approach, in one paragraph:** We already have buyers, so we do not start cold. We start by squeezing proof (testimonials, results, story) out of existing buyers, sell a **Founding 25** tier to the warm circle by direct outreach in the first 3 weeks, then run a public content engine (daily Afrofuturist short-form + weekly free masterclass funnel) to fill the remaining 50 seats, and deliver the cohort loudly in public so the hackathon finale becomes the marketing engine for cohort 2. Full-time capacity means the content engine runs **daily, without exception** — the AI produces, Mart approves and appears on camera.

**North-star metric:** paying members (weekly count).
**Supporting metrics (tracked weekly):** email list size, masterclass registrations, sales-call/DM conversations started, short-form views.

### Top risks and countermeasures
1. **Mart's on-camera time becomes the bottleneck.** Countermeasure: batch recording (one sitting per week), and the brand-visual reel format (voiceover over cosmic footage) as the no-camera fallback so the daily streak never breaks.
2. **Founding 25 stalls below ~15.** That's a signal, not a failure: pause Phase 3 spend, run 5 customer conversations to find the objection, fix the offer one-pager, then resume. Do not push ads at an offer the warm circle wouldn't buy.
3. **Toolkit is thin at launch → refunds and churn.** Countermeasure: AC on task 1.5 (Mart personally uses 2 assets) is non-negotiable; add one new Toolkit asset every week of the cohort.
4. **Content engine drifts into generic AI-guru content.** Countermeasure: the brand-voice rules at the top of this doc; weekly review checks the last 7 posts against them. Afrofuturist identity is the differentiator — protect it.
5. **The $15M/3,000-member framing leaks into public copy.** Never publish aspirational internal numbers. Public claims only cite real results with permission.
6. **Delivery eats selling (or vice versa) in Weeks 5–8.** Countermeasure: the daily rhythm below hard-allocates both; the AI flags any week where either publishing or live labs slipped.
